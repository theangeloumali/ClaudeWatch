Campus models

campus-furniture.glb is baked from "Furniture Kit" (2.0) and campus-person.glb from
"Mini Characters" (1.0), both created and distributed by Kenney (www.kenney.nl).

License: Creative Commons Zero, CC0
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The bake flattens each model to one untextured mesh with its colours in the vertices, and
splits the character into one rigid mesh per joint. Thanks to Kenney for the packs.
