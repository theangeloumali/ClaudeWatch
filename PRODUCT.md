# ClaudeWatch

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

The React/WebGL Office runs in an Electron desktop application and a browser surface.

## Users

Owners supervising software agents across multiple live organizations and projects.

## Product purpose

Make actual session activity, attention needs and project relationships visible, with access to the existing session inspector and supported terminal/actions.

## Operating context

Claude, Codex and OpenCode sessions determine workers and their workspaces. Decorative people and buildings provide context; they are not employee records or monitored work.

## Capabilities and constraints

Preserve session identity, attention behavior, action authorization, company/project theme inheritance, List fallback, reduced motion and quality controls. Keep orthographic rendering and deterministic town layout; the 35-degree reference view is a preset alongside bounded owner-controlled pan, zoom and orbit.

## Brand commitments

Angelo approved a contemporary Corporate City world, human-looking software agents and Cities: Skylines-style readable zoning on 2026-10-13. Keep existing worlds selectable and saved choices intact. Do not copy game assets or create a city simulator.

The additional Samurai Empire world uses Japanese-inspired cyberpunk architecture and human samurai with cybernetic accents. Monitors report actual activity; terminal Peek remains explicitly requested in the inspector. Office and screen-side workstation presets yield to manual camera movement.

## Evidence on hand

The approved whole-feature plan is `docs/plans/claudewatch-office-city-corporate-redesign-2026-10-13.md`. Existing source and fixture captures document the current Office; no real-person likeness reference is supplied.

## Product principles

- Appearance never creates a new identity or claims a human is staffing a software session.
- Visual activity follows actual session state.
- Operational access must not require walking or precise canvas interaction.
- Model detail remains bounded by the existing rendering budgets.
